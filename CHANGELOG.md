# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first Bedrock release of Little Birds, with everything in the Java edition's 0.3.0.
Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.60 or newer, no experiments.

- Five birds: the budgie (four colours), the cockatiel (three, with a crest that stands up when it
  whistles hello), the lovebird (three, lives in a pair that shares one perch), the canary (yellow,
  red, white; sings every morning) and the zebra finch (grey, fawn, white; keeps company and
  answers a finch that sings). Tamed with seeds; each with its own recorded voice. Wild in
  savannas, jungle edges, flower forests, meadows, cherry groves and plains.
- The perch, crafted from string, a stick and a plank: a tamed bird makes the nearest one its home,
  sits on it by day, sleeps on it at night, and stays home when you go out. Use it to call your
  bird over.
- The greeting: back after an hour or more away, your bird says hello.
- Shoulder on request: sneak and use a tamed budgie, cockatiel or lovebird and it rides your
  shoulder through jumps, falls and portals.
- Chicks: two tamed birds of a kind fed seeds make a chick in a parent's colour that grows up in
  about twenty minutes. Lovebirds breed only with their partner.
- The millet spray (wheat and seeds): tames a wild bird on the first try, draws a bird to your
  hand, and gets a happy bob or a song. Farm snacks for tamed birds; cocoa beans and poisonous
  potatoes politely refused.
- Settings as a form: `/little_birds:settings` (operators) turns wild spawning off or makes it
  rarer.
- On touch controls, use a bird with the interact button above the hotbar (tapping attacks).

How it differs from the Java edition is in the README.
